Robotics & AV Club

The Robotics & AV Club, where technology meets creativity! We design and build cutting-edge robots for competitions. Our team focuses on energy efficient designs, and smart automation to drive sustainability forward. Through hackathons, techfests, maker fests, and industry collaborations, we connect with experts and shape the future of robotics and AV technology. Join us in building smarter, greener solutions!

Vision

To emerge as a centre of excellence in Mechanical Engineering Education, Research and Innovations for Sustainable Development.

MISSION

1. Build ecosystem for academic excellence and innovative research in Technology and Engineering. 2. Foster Collaborations with Institutes and Industries. 3. Contribute towards holistic and sustainable development.
